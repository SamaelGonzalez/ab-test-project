# ab-test-project
Analysis of a marketing A/B test where the majority of people will be exposed to ads (treatment group), and a small portion of people (control group) would instead see a Public Service ANnouncement (PSA) or nothing in the exact size and place the ad would normally be.

Data was taken from Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/faviovaz/marketing-ab-testing

We go through standard statistical pipeline: SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch), EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis), MDE (Minimum Detectable Effect) check, Hypothesis Test using Two Proportion Z-Test, Effect Size calculation, Regression robustness check and Business practical recommendation.
